## Letter 1 — Kabir, after the paediatrician visit
Community Paediatrics Clinic, Riverside Health Centre
Seen: 14 September 2026

Re: Kabir, DOB (age 30 months)

Thank you for seeing this 2.5-year-old boy, referred by his GP with global developmental delay and feeding concerns.

Hx: Hypotonic infant. Sat unsupported at 14/12, not yet walking independently, cruises with 2 hands held. ~5 single words. Slow feeder, coughs and chokes on lumpy textures, on purées. 4 chest infections in the last 12 months. Intermittent R convergent squint when tired. Murmur noted at 6-week check, no follow-up.

O/E: Weight and height both <3rd centile. OFC <3rd centile (microcephaly). Marked generalised hypotonia with normal reflexes, no focal weakness. Mild upslanting palpebral fissures and a high arched palate. Grade 2/6 systolic murmur at the left sternal edge. No hepatosplenomegaly. No seizures reported. No regression, skills have been slowly gained. Hearing screen at birth passed; tympanograms normal today. No skin lesions.

Impression: Global developmental delay with hypotonia, feeding difficulty, short stature, microcephaly and a cardiac murmur. Unexplained.

Plan: Echocardiogram. Speech and language therapy, feeding assessment. Dietitian. Will discuss at the multidisciplinary meeting. Review in 3 months.

Dr A. Example, Consultant Paediatrician
