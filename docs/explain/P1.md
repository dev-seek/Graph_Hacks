# P1 in plain English: the knowledge graph

**What this phase built.** The medical memory the whole product reads from, loaded into FalkorDB from public files.

- **Data download** (`make data`). Five files pinned by version and checksum: the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO, release 2026-09-01) and its disease annotations, Orphanet's prevalence and ID-alignment files, and Phenopacket Store 0.1.27 (published, de-identified case reports we use for testing). Nothing raw is committed.
- **Readers for each file** (`backend/app/kb/parsers/`). Written test-first against a tiny made-up ontology with fake IDs. They turn the files into plain records: terms and their parent links, disease–sign links with how often the sign occurs, "this disease does NOT have this sign" links, rarity classes turned into rough numbers, and links between Orphanet and OMIM disease IDs.
- **The knowledge graph** (`make kb`). 19,894 signs/symptoms, 20,727 diseases (12,062 with at least one sign recorded), 237,270 disease–sign links and 8,745 Orphanet↔OMIM links. For each sign we store how deep it sits in the tree, how common it is across diseases (`bg`), how informative it is (`ic`) and which body system it belongs to. Search indexes (exact words, fuzzy words and meaning-based) are built on the sign names. Building from scratch takes about two minutes, about 20 seconds when the meaning-vectors are cached.
- **OMIM licence rule.** 27,851 OMIM rows that were only computer-inferred (IEA) are left out unless a setting says otherwise. HPO's own text is never changed; our additions sit in separate `cx_*` fields.
- **Words to signs** (`resolve.py`). A parent's words ("squint", "throwing up") are matched to HPO terms by graph search plus meaning vectors. Official lay synonyms: 50 of 50 found in the top three. Whole sentences written by us as a harder test: 9 of 20 (45%); the language model plus the parent's confirmation handles sentences later.
- **Plain-language labels** for each sign (ask at home / doctor checks / needs a test; youngest sensible age; a question). No API key was set, so these are rule-based only (15,362 home, 3,614 test, 201 exam); a 100-term spot-check sheet is waiting for the human.
- **Frozen test split.** Cases are split by the paper they came from (dev 2,881, test 7,496 cases, 1,197 test papers). The test list's checksum is in the README. A second graph, `kb_20260901_lpo`, leaves out annotations that cite any test paper, so testing cannot cheat by reading the answer.
- **Coverage.** 99.7% of the 780 diagnoses in the case set land on a disease with recorded signs. Under the leave-paper-out graph only 68.7% of test cases still do, because the paper that described a disease is often its only source.
- **Licence text** for HPO and Phenopacket Store copied verbatim into `docs/DATA_LICENSES.md`. `/api/version` reports every data version.

**What it does not do yet.** No scoring, no questions to parents, no safety on live messages; those start in P2.

**Things worth knowing.**
- Unmatched signs default to "ask at home", so some odd ones (a fractured heel bone) are askable; P2's planner must filter by age, depth and usefulness.
- Some rule-written questions use a lay synonym that is itself a condition word (e.g. "Epilepsy"); the P3 phrasing check must catch banned words before anything reaches a parent.
